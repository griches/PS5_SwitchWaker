Controller prompts by Xelu
https://thoseawesomeguys.com/prompts/

Released under Creative Commons CC0 1.0 Universal (public domain dedication):
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

The original PS5 PNGs are in ps5/assets/controller-ui/xelu/.
ProsperoWake converts these to game textures and an ImGui atlas. Its layout
instructions are applied to game resources loaded from the player's disc.
The camera-eye artwork is also read from that disc and combined with Xelu's R3
prompt at runtime. No original game layouts or camera artwork are bundled.

The zoom arrows are generated artwork made for ProsperoWake, not Xelu assets.
Their generator and artwork are provided under the MIT licence (MIT.txt).
